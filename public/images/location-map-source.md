Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BlankMap-USA-states-Canada-provinces.svg

Wikimedia Commons identifies this map as public domain (PD-map).

Changes: dark land fill (#25384b), transparent background, and a Wisconsin-centered
viewBox (1437.25 1074.625 362.5 293.75). Original paths and the gaps between them are preserved.
The lighter tile background (#71889a) shows through those gaps and water areas.
